# Wild Crystal

**Johto, with everything the suite learned on Kanto.**

Crystal is already the best of the three Gen 2 carts — the animated sprites,
the girl you can play as, the Battle Tower, Suicune's whole story. It never
needed rescuing the way Red did. So this is not a rescue: it is the Gen1Wild
suite brought over to a game that was already good, doing the things Crystal's
own menus never got around to.

A #DEX you can open an undiscovered POKéMON in, with its name masked to dashes
and its nests still shown on the map, so you can go and look for it. A storage
box you can actually arrange, with gaps where you want them. A party that
carries a POKéMON through the list instead of swapping two. Battle backdrops
drawn for Johto's own towns. Sprinting, autosave, auto continue, a POKéMON
walking behind you.

**And you can catch every single one of them.** All 251, in one save, on one
cartridge, without trading. The trade evolutions are handled, the roamers are
catchable, the legendaries go back where they were if you knock them out, and
the GS BALL finally hands over the CELEBI event that shipped finished and
unreachable everywhere outside Japan.

## It plays as its own version

Wild Crystal is a **custom cart**: a named, fixed set of mods that plays as its
own game, with its own entry in the launcher, its own cartridge and its own
save slots. Nothing here writes into your base Crystal saves. Two people
running Wild Crystal are running the same game.

It pins two things: **Gen1WildQOL** and **Gen1WildUI**, the two halves of the
suite. Two rather than Wild Green's four — Crystal ships its own animated
battle sprites and its own shiny reveals, so the mod Green pins for those has
nothing to do here, and there is no player recolour because the cart already
lets you choose.

The set is sealed, which is what lets it go online: both sides have to be
running the same game. Every feature inside the two is still a switch you can
flip.

## Trading with Wild Green

You can move POKéMON between this cart and **Wild Green**, and it is the Time
Capsule that does it — save to save, not over a live cable.

Going up to Crystal, a Kanto POKéMON arrives with its level, its nickname and
its stat experience intact. Coming back down, the cartridge's own rules apply:
no Johto POKéMON, no moves Gen 1 never had, and nothing holding MAIL. Each
refusal names itself before anything moves.

The base game is **Crystal**. The cart carries no game data and no ROM bytes.
