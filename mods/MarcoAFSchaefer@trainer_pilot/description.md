# Trainer Pilot

A training and hunting bot for FireRed and LeafGreen. It plays by pressing the game's own buttons, and any button you press pauses it.

- **Routes**: grass routes recorded by walking them; fishing, VS Seeker and Sweet Scent spots marked where you stand.
- **Training**: fights with the party members you choose, with moves you can forbid; stops at a target level, a shiny, a species from your list, a new move or an evolution.
- **EV training**: a target per stat and per Pokémon; runs from species that would give the wrong EVs.
- **Healing**: walks to the nearest Pokémon Center through a map of how the world's maps connect, crossing Surf, Cut, Rock Smash, Strength and Waterfall obstacles, or uses Potions and Ethers from the bag up to a limit.
- **Hunting**: up to 30 targets filtered by species, gender, nature, ability, IVs, Hidden Power, level, held item and shiny; optional automatic catching; the Safari Zone.
- **Reset hunting** for starters, gifts and legendaries: saves in front of the Pokémon and reloads until it matches. While it runs, nothing else may write the save.
- Stop history, game speed while running, and the numbers of every run.

The control panel lives in **Trainer Desk**, so that mod is needed to use it. Panel in English, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese.

**Known limits:** puzzle caves (Victory Road, Seafoam Islands and a few more) are never crossed; in double battles only the first opponent is targeted; item healing restores HP and PP but not status.

Tested on FireRed and LeafGreen with Gen1Recomp 0.4.8.
