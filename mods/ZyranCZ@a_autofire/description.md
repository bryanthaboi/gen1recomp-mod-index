Autofire AB adds configurable autofire support to Gen1Recomp for Pokémon Red, Blue, Yellow, and Gold. Hold A, B, or optionally the directional buttons to automatically repeat inputs, making dialogue, battles, and menus easier to navigate without repeatedly pressing buttons.

Choose which face buttons use autofire, enable or disable D-pad autofire separately, set the hold delay and repeat rate, and restrict autofire to everywhere, battles only, or the overworld only.
