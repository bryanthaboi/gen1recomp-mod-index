# Trainer Desk

Turns the game window into an IDE-style workspace: the game stays in the middle, and panels supplied by other mods sit in docks around it.

- Docks on the left, right and bottom, with tabs and draggable dividers.
- Drag a tab to move a panel to another dock; close it, or open it in its own desktop window.
- Panels that draw themselves at any size get exactly the room their dock has.
- The picture another mod sends to the second screen can be docked as a panel too.
- Themes, a hide/show key (F10 by default), and a layout that is remembered between sessions.
- Menus in English, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese.

Trainer Desk shows nothing by itself: it is the home for panels such as those of **Trainer Lens** and **Trainer Pilot**. Other mods can add their own through a small API, documented in the repository (`docs/API.md`).

Tested on FireRed and LeafGreen with Gen1Recomp 0.4.8.
