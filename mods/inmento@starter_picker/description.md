# Starter Picker

Starter Picker lets players choose starters by original ball position in Gen 1 and Gold. Each ball has an independent selector, and the normal rival counter-pick relationship remains intact.

Gen 1 covers Oak’s Lab in Red and Blue with all 151 species. Gold covers Elm’s Lab with all 251 species, including named and weighted held-item choices for the player starter. Gold also offers player-only Preserve, Max, Random, and legal Shiny DV modes; the rival’s DVs and held item remain independent.

The mod supports the authorized Gen 1 and Gold Randomizer compatibility paths. A player’s explicit ball selection remains the final player-starter choice when a supported Randomizer starter mode is active.
