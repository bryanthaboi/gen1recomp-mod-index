Adds a male/female symbol (♂/♀) to the level display, matching how Gold/Silver/Crystal show gender info.

Shows the symbol right before the level in battle (both layouts, both your Pokemon and the opponent's)
- Shows it right before the level on the individual Pokemon summary/stats
  screen
- Keeps the "LV" tile visible on the summary screen even at level 100 (real vanilla hides it there to make room for the third digit)

Genderless species and Nidoran (whose name already shows its gender)correctly get no extra symbol.
