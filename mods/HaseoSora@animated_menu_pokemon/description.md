Kanto in Motion is a visual overhaul for Gen1Recomp supporting Pokémon Red/Blue/Yellow, Gold/Silver/Crystal, and FireRed/LeafGreen/Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald. It adds HD animated Pokémon sprites, modern battle and menu interfaces, redesigned Pokédex and party screens, HD battle backgrounds, and extensive UI customization.

The large HD battle Pokémon sprite sheets and backgrounds are distributed as an **optional separate asset download**, not included in the core mod ZIP. On first launch, KIM's HD Asset Manager offers to download the official Kanto in Motion Assets pack. You can choose **USE VANILLA / LATER** instead. Downloaded assets are cached and reused across normal KIM updates; the asset manager also supports later downloads and updates.

Source and installation details: https://github.com/HaseoSora/Kanto-in-Motion
