Battle Factory Remix brings the Battle Factory experience to Pokémon Red and Crystal through Gen1Recomp.

Build a team from rental Pokémon, battle through a series of Factory opponents, and swap one of your rentals for a defeated opponent's Pokémon after each victory. Successful runs lead to challenging Factory Head battles and earn Battle Points that can be spent on unique rewards.

## Features

* Battle Factory challenges for Pokémon Red and Crystal
* Choose a team from randomized rental Pokémon
* Swap rentals with defeated opponents between battles
* Game-specific Factory challenges and opponents
* Factory Head battles
* Battle Point rewards and prize shop
* Optional PokeSurvive compatibility
* Designed as a self-contained side activity that can be added to a normal playthrough

Each supported game has its own implementation while sharing the core Battle Factory experience.
