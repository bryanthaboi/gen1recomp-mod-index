GameShark Compatibility brings a GameShark-style cheat and debug menu to Gen1Recomp across Gen 1 and Gen 2.
It supports Pokémon Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, and Silver and automatically adapts its features to the active game.
Features include walk through walls, no random battles, infinite HP and PP, one-hit KO, max money and Game Corner coins, Master Balls and Rare Candies, badge cheats, trainer Pokémon capture, configurable wild Pokémon encounters, instant battles, teleportation, Give Item, unrestricted Teach Move, and a DV/EV editor.
Wild Pokémon controls include species and level selection, with Gen 2 gender and shiny controls. The Teleport feature allows travel to major destinations without normal Fly restrictions. Give Item can add items and quantities directly to the bag, while Teach Move can assign any available move to any party Pokémon without normal learnset restrictions.
GameShark is standalone and does not require other mods.
The mod uses Gen1Recomp's Mod API 2 and is distributed through GitHub Releases. Once installed, Gen1Recomp can use the repository metadata for Update and Versions support.
Supported games: Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, and Silver.
Source and releases: goofwear/gameshark
