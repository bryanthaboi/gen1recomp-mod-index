Every Pokémon you catch gets a name of its own, picked from themed sets: people, pets, food, baked goods, nature, myths, space, names that suit a certain type or species, and nearly 300 canon nicknames from the games, anime and manga. A Fire type might be Cinder, a Ninetales Kyubi, a Pikachu Volty. Pop culture and meme names are there too, switched off to start.
 
Trainers' Pokémon can have names too. Gym leaders bring their canon names into battle: Lt. Surge sends out Volty, Jasmine sends out Rusty, and Red on Mt. Silver sends out Pika, Vee, Snor and Saur.
 
## What it does
 
- **Catches:** named the moment they're caught. Names follow the Pokémon's gender, a name already in your party or boxes isn't picked again, and the Name Rater still works if you have a better one.
- **Sets:** every set has its own ON/OFF row, so leaving only **BAKED GOODS** on gives you a bakery team.
- **TRAINER TEAMS:** **CANON** gives canon trainers their names, **ALL** names every trainer's team from your sets (the same way every rematch), **OFF** leaves them alone.
- **LETTERS:** **CAPS** (MABEL) like the game's own names, or **Mixed** (Mabel).
- **GIFTS:** starters and other gifts are left for you to name, unless you set them to **AUTO**.
- **Your own lists:** add a text file to `names/` and it gets its own row. Lists can give names to a type, a species, or a specific trainer's Pokémon. The README has the format.
- **Achievements:** with [Recomp Achievements](https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/recomp_achievements) installed, 21 achievements on their own **NAME TAGS** tab, for your catches and for meeting canon trainers' named Pokémon. It's optional.
## Install
 
1. Download `name_tags-<version>.zip` from the [releases page](https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/name_tags/releases).
2. In the launcher, MODS → **Import mod .zip**.
3. Enable it and load your game.
To update, import the new zip over the old one. Your settings carry over.
 
## Compatibility
 
- Mod API 2, gen1recomp 0.4.4 or newer. Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver, Crystal, FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald.
- `content` profile: link play is unaffected.
- Naming uses its own random numbers, so battles and encounters play out the same.
- In-game trades keep their names, and a catch another mod has already named is left alone.
