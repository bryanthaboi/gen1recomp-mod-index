PhasmoPokéa brings paranormal investigations to Pokémon Crystal.

Travel to Lavender Town and join Ezra as a Spirit Warden, taking on nighttime cases in haunted locations. Track an invisible roaming spirit, investigate environmental disturbances, gather clues with the Spirit Band, identify the haunting Pokémon, locate the object anchoring it to the area, and perform a successful cleansing before the investigation turns deadly.

Investigations feature darkness and restricted visibility, species-specific spirit behavior, environmental clues, first-person inspection scenes, selectable Warden Tools, dangerous wrong-seal chase sequences, and permanent Warden rank progression.

As your rank increases, new equipment becomes available, including Binding Ash, Warding Charms, Powerlights, Thermometers, Silph Sensors, UV Lights, and additional equipment slots.
