# Trainer Controls

Map every control the way you want to play, on keyboard, mouse and gamepad.

- **Game buttons**: D-pad, A, B, START, SELECT, L and R.
- **Game functions**: speed up and down, quick save and load, zoom, tilt.
- **Field shortcuts** that skip the menus: Cut, Surf, Fly, Strength, Flash, Rock Smash, Waterfall, Dig, Teleport, Sweet Scent, Bicycle, Itemfinder and VS Seeker. Each works only when the game would allow it (badge, a Pokémon that knows the move, the item, the place). A shortcut can share its button with L or R (R1 for Cut while walking, R in the Bag); inputs that really clash are marked.
- **Two keys or mouse buttons and two gamepad buttons per action**, including triggers, both sticks, the mouse wheel and the DualSense touchpad click.
- **PlayStation, Xbox and Nintendo button names**, picked from the connected gamepad or set by hand.
- **A controls screen over the game** (F8), usable with gamepad, keyboard or mouse, and the same list as a **Trainer Desk** panel.
- Other mods can add their own actions to the list.

English, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese. Tested on FireRed and LeafGreen with Gen1Recomp 0.4.8.
