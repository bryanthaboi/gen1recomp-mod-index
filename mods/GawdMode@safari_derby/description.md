Safari Derby transforms Fuchsia City's former Safari Zone into a new Pokémon racing, training, wagering, and prize side activity for Pokémon Crystal.

Enter your own Pokémon in races, manage their stamina and performance on the track, wager on competing racers, train your favorites, and work your way through increasingly challenging Derby Cups.

## Features

- Pokémon racing at the former Fuchsia Safari Zone
- Enter eligible party Pokémon as racers
- Multiple racing cups with different entry requirements
- Active racing with player-controlled gallops
- Pokémon-specific racing performance
- Training and progression for your racers
- Spectator races with Wager Point betting
- Odds, payouts, and betting streaks
- Wager Point prize shop with unique rewards
- Derby ribbons for winning major races
- Persistent racing records and progression
- Custom race presentation, announcer commentary, crowds, and track visuals

Build your racer, pick your moments to gallop, and prove which Pokémon is fastest in Fuchsia.
