 Features
98 Mega form entries and 92 Mega Stones, plus the Key Stone.
In-battle Mega Evolution — activate the Mega button, choose a move and transform before attacking.
Animated transformations featuring the Mega symbol, white-flash reveal and Mega Pokémon cries.
Updated Mega stats, types and abilities, with temporary forms that last until fainting or the end of battle.
Normal and shiny Mega sprites, including battle artwork, HP-bar indicators and party icons.
Mega Stone shops in Professor Oak's and Professor Birch's laboratories.
Mega-powered trainers and bosses, including selected Gym Leaders, Elite Four members, rivals and villains.
Trainer Mega Audit, for checking boss encounters and compatible Pokémon teams.
Optional compatibility with National Dex Gen 3, G9 Battle Sprites, Gen 3 HD Sprites, Modern Spawns, Untamed Advanced and Nuzlocke.

 How to play
Install the release ZIP through Gen1Recomp's MODS menu, buy a Key Stone and the appropriate Mega Stone from a professor's laboratory, then press START in battle while selecting a move to activate Mega Evolution!

One Mega Evolution is allowed per side, per battle.
