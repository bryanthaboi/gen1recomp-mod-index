# Intro Bypass

Skip Oak's intro: pick your name, starter (starters + legendaries), shiny or
not, and level, then jump straight into the overworld.

## What you get

- **Skip the Oak speech** from the title menu — no more mashing through the
  intro every new save.
- **Full starter choice**: the usual starters plus legendaries.
- **Shiny toggle** and **level pick** for your starter before you start.
- **Straight to the overworld** once you confirm your choices.

## Install

Download the zip from the [releases page](https://github.com/hdbreaker/intro_bypass/releases)
and import it with **F10** in-game, or unzip it into your `mods/` folder.

## Usage

From the title menu, select the new **INTRO BYPASS** option, make your
choices, and confirm. The game drops you into the overworld with your chosen
starter.

## Requirements

- Gen1Recomp with mod API 2 (`api: 2`)
- `engine_internals` permission (declared in the manifest)
