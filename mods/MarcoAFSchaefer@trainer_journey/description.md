# Trainer Journey

A journey guide for FireRed and LeafGreen that reads your save and tells you where to go next.

- **Next step**: the current objective, where to go and what helps there, plus what you can do on the way: Pokédex gifts waiting, items you can already pick up, HMs nobody in your party knows yet, side trips.
- **Story**: 28 steps from your first Pokémon to the Champion, and the optional Sevii Islands trip.
- **HMs & key items**: where each one is, from when, and whether its move works outside battle with your badges.
- **Obstacles**: what blocks the way and what is still missing for each.
- **Post-game**: National Pokédex, Celio's network, Mewtwo and the legendary birds.
- **Pokédex rewards** from Professor Oak's aides.
- **An arrow on the game screen** points the way to the objective: the door, the edge of the map or the person to talk to, with the number of steps. It follows the real connections between maps.
- **Spoiler-free by default**: upcoming steps stay hidden until you switch them on.

The panels live in **Trainer Desk**, so that mod is needed to see them. English, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese.

Tested on FireRed and LeafGreen with Gen1Recomp 0.4.8.
