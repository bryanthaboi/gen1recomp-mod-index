# Pikachu True Potential ⚡

Let your **Pokémon Yellow** partner Pikachu reach its true potential—without evolving!

Oak's starter Pikachu grows gradually from normal Pikachu base stats at level 5 to **Raichu's base stats at level 30**, while keeping its Pikachu identity, follower interactions, happiness mechanics, and Pokédex #025 identity. **Only Oak's starter** receives these changes; wild and traded Pikachu and Raichu remain unchanged.

## Features

- **Stronger partner, no evolution:** Base stats increase between levels 5 and 30, then remain at Raichu base-stat values.
- **Double Kick at level 9:** Added alongside the original level-up move.
- **Amnesia at level 33:** Replaces Agility for the special partner.
- **Extra TM/HM compatibility:** HM02 Fly, HM03 Surf, HM04 Strength, TM26 Earthquake, and TM28 Dig.
- **Classic Yellow partner experience:** Pikachu still follows you and can be nicknamed.

Normal badge requirements still apply to field HM use. Previously learned moves are not changed retroactively.

## Compatibility and installation

Requires **Pokémon Yellow** imported into **G1R Deluxe / Gen1Recomp**. Install and enable the mod, then receive your starter Pikachu from Professor Oak. The mod uses the `engine_internals` permission.

Compatibility with **Crystal Animated Sprites with Shiny Visuals** has been gameplay-tested (against its v2.0.3 source). Other mod combinations are not guaranteed. **Restart G1R Deluxe after enabling or disabling mods**; hot unload/reload is not fully supported.

This mod does not include ROM data or extracted game assets.
