🦌 Wild Pokémon in the overworld: Every area with wild encounters fills with visible Pokémon.
🤝 A partner with a job: Choose any Pokémon in your party menu and give it a role. It can follow you, forage for items as you walk, or battle the wild Pokémon around you.
🎲 Classic encounters, your call: Keep the original step-based encounters alongside the visible ones, or turn them off and only meet what you can see.
🫂 Friendship you can see: Face your partner and press A to Pet, Play fetch or Talk. Each one raises its friendship, and its portrait shows how it really feels from its HP, status and bond with you.
⚔️ Battler: Stand still near a wild Pokémon and your partner charges in. It fights with its real moves, stats and PP, on land or swimming out over water, and earns EXP.
🍂 Forager: On routes and in caves, every 1-3 minutes of walking it catches a scent, dashes to a glint on the ground and always digs something up.
🌊 Water that feels like water: wild Pokémon in the water swim about, bob gently on the surface, leave a ring of wake behind them, and dive under water.
🏃 They notice you: run or bike close by and wild Pokémon react. Weaker Pokemon run, stronger ones attack.
🕊️ Birds in the sky: flying mon act like real birds. They fly overhead, land, perch, and fly away when spooked.
📏 True-to-life sizes (with Gen 3 HD Sprites): every species is drawn at its own relative size, smoothly, at your screen's resolution.
