Formerly Wilds of Hoenn.

- Visible wild Pokémon in grass, water and caves, from the game's own encounter tables. Walk onto one to battle it.
- Classic step encounters optional.
- A partner from your party that follows you, forages for items or battles wild Pokémon for EXP.
- Pet, Play or Talk with your partner to raise friendship; its portrait shows its mood.
- Wild Pokémon behave like their species: they flee, chase, move in groups, sleep, burrow and hunt.
- Water life, birds in the sky and signature effects for almost 100 Pokémon.
- HGSS / PokeMMO or animated PMDCollab sprites, with true-to-life sizes through WoobleView.
