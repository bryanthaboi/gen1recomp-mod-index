*Formerly Wilds of Hoenn.*

🦌 Wild Pokémon in the overworld: grass, water and caves fill with visible wild Pokémon from the game's own encounter tables. Walk onto one to battle it. The Safari Zone works too.
🎲 Classic encounters, your call: keep the original step-based encounters alongside the visible ones, or turn them off and only meet what you can see.
🤝 A partner with a job: choose any Pokémon in your party menu and give it a role. It can follow you, forage for items as you walk, or battle the wild Pokémon around you.
🫂 Friendship you can see: face your partner and press A to Pet, Play fetch or Talk. Each one raises its friendship, and its portrait shows how it really feels from its HP, status and bond with you.
⚔️ Battler: stand still near a wild Pokémon and your partner charges in. It fights with its real moves, stats and PP, on land or swimming out over water, and earns EXP.
🍂 Forager: on routes and in caves, every 1-3 minutes of walking it catches a scent, dashes to a glint on the ground and always digs something up.
🏃 Pokecology: wild Pokémon behave like their species. Skittish ones flee, territorial ones chase you, packs, herds and schools move together, some sleep by night or by day, diggers burrow, hunters chase prey, and some glow after dark.
🌊 Water that feels like water: swimmers bob, leave a wake and dive, fish leap, Wailmer spout, and Finizen porpoise out at sea.
🕊️ Birds in the sky: birds fly overhead, perch on roofs and dive for fish. Some come down to be met, and a sea bird holding its catch is met together with that fish in a wild double battle.
🔥 Signature effects: almost 100 Pokémon carry their own living effect, like Charmander's tail flame, Pikachu's crackling cheeks and Gastly's drifting gas.
🎨 Two sprite styles: HGSS / PokeMMO art or animated PMDCollab sprites, with alternate and regional forms.
📏 True-to-life sizes (with WoobleView): every species is drawn at its own relative size, smoothly, at your screen's resolution.
