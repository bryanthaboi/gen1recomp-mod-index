New looks for everyone but you, in all eleven of gen1recomp's games: FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, and Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal. The people on the map, the trainers and Pokémon you battle, party icons, battle scenes and boxes, windows and the whole screen can each get a new look, one option at a time. Anything you don't change stays the game's own.

Facets leaves the player and the rival alone: [Emerald Lens](https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/emerald_lens) draws them in the Gen 3 games and [Crystal Lens](https://github.com/dburton95/crystal) in the Game Boy games. They never touch the same sprites, so they work together in either load order, and share one **PRISM** item on the START and OPTION menus.

## FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald

- **NPCS:** four included sets (COMMUNITY, DIEGO, TAIGA and ZAFFRE) that together give every person in all five games new art. **GBC** and **GBC 2X** give everyone a Game Boy Color look, from your own imported Gen 1/2 games plus Gen 2-style art for everyone the Game Boy games never had.
- **TRAINERS:** **DS** (Gen 4+ style), **GBC-ISH** and **GAME BOY**.
- **POKEMON:** **DS**, **GBC-ISH**, **GBC ANIM** and **GB EMERALD**. **FR/LG ANIMS** gives FireRed and LeafGreen Emerald's front animations, and **YOUR SIDE** shows your own Pokémon by their front picture.
- **BATTLE UI:** battle boxes, HP bars and text boxes in XY, B2W2, DP, Platinum, HGSS, BW, Gaia and more, or Gen 2's own.
- **WINDOWS**, **BATTLE BG** (twenty scenes, or Crystal's plain white) and **PREBUILD**, which builds pictures ahead of time for small boards and handhelds.

## Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal

- **NPCS:** the same four sets, walking on Gen 3's steps, with fill-in sets so every person has art: JOHTO DS for Johto's leaders, Elite Four and story people, DIONYUS1 for Yellow's Jessie, James and Officer Jenny.
- **TRAINERS** and **POKEMON:** **DS** for every trainer class and Pokémon, or **FIRERED** and **EMERALD** read from your own imported games, with Emerald's animations on Gold, Silver and Crystal. **CRYSTAL** gives Red, Blue and Yellow Crystal's sprites and icons.
- **PIKACHU** (Yellow): seven looks for your partner, on the map, in battle, on its summary and in the Hall of Fame.
- **SIZE** for Pokémon and for trainers, each on its own: FIT, FULL, SMALL or TINY.
- **BATTLE BG: SCENERY** picked from where you are, **BATTLE UI** sets in wide battles, **WINDOWS** (FireRed's frames or a TITO style), **ICONS: MINIDEX** and **COLORS**, which keeps the game in full colour while anything in Facets is on.
- A **CREDITS** page on the FACETS screen names everyone whose art you can see.

## Everywhere

- **FILTER:** GRAY, GREEN, YELLOW, GOLD, CRYSTAL, RUBY, SAPPHIRE, EMERALD or GBC, on the whole screen or just the map, with DOTS and 4, 8 or 16 SHADES.
- **Achievements:** with Recomp Achievements installed, Facets adds its own FACETS tab of looks to try. Each one shows only in the games it can be earned in.

## Install

1. Download `facets-<version>.zip` from the [releases page](https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/facets/releases).
2. In the launcher, MODS → **Import mod .zip**.
3. Enable it and load your game. Everything stays the game's own until you pick a look.

To update, import the new zip over the old one. Your settings carry over.

The GBC, GBC-ISH, GB EMERALD, FIRERED, EMERALD and CRYSTAL looks are made from your own imported games while you play, so they need those games imported; the FACETS screen says when one is missing. Nothing from any game is included.

## Compatibility

- Mod API 2, gen1recomp 0.3.98 or newer. All eleven games.
- `content` profile: link play is unaffected.
- Works alongside Emerald Lens, Crystal Lens, Recomp Achievements and SPRITE SWAPPER (and can make SPRITE SWAPPER packs from its sets).
