New looks for FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald: every NPC on the overworld, every trainer you battle and every Pokémon, plus battle boxes, windows, battle scenes and Game Boy screen filters. Pick a look per part, mix them however you like, and anything a set doesn't cover stays the game's own.

Facets is the companion to [Emerald Lens](https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/emerald_lens): Emerald Lens does the player and the rival, Facets does everyone else. They never touch the same sprites, so they work together in either load order.

## What it changes

- **NPCs:** four included sprite sets (DIEGO, TAIGA, ZAFFRE and COMMUNITY) that together give every person in all five games new art: every trainer, townsperson, staff member and story character. **GBC** and **GBC 2X** give everyone a Game Boy Color look, from your own imported Gen 1/2 games plus Gen 2-style art for everyone the Game Boy games never had.
- **TRAINERS:** new battle pictures for every trainer you face. **DS** (Gen 4+ style), **GBC-ISH** (Crystal's and Yellow's trainers from your own games, plus hand-made Gen 2-style art for Hoenn) and **GAME BOY**.
- **POKéMON:** pictures, party icons and animations. **DS** (the DS-style 64x64 resource), **GBC-ISH**, **GBC ANIM** (Crystal's animated sprites with Nuuk's animated Hoenn) and **GB EMERALD**. **FR/LG ANIMS** gives FireRed and LeafGreen Emerald's front animations, and **YOUR SIDE** shows your own Pokémon by their front picture.
- **BATTLE UI:** battle boxes, HP bars and text boxes in XY, B2W2, DP, Platinum, HGSS, BW, Gaia and more, or Gen 2's own HP bars.
- **WINDOWS:** Crystal's eight window frames, or Tito Seich's text boxes.
- **BATTLE BG:** twenty new battle scenes from the Battle Backgrounds Patch FR, or Crystal's plain white.
- **FILTER:** Game Boy greys or greens, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald shades, a GBC colour look and a dot-matrix screen.
- **PREBUILD:** builds and keeps pictures ahead of time for small boards and handhelds, so nothing hitches mid-battle.

Everything is set from **PRISM** on the START and OPTION menus (shared with Emerald Lens), with live previews, or from the mod's options.

## Install

1. Download `facets-<version>.zip` from the [releases page](https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/facets/releases).
2. In the launcher, MODS → **Import mod .zip**.
3. Enable it and load your game. Everything stays the game's own until you pick a look.

The GBC, GBC-ISH and GB EMERALD looks and FR/LG ANIMS are made from your own imported games while you play (Crystal, Gold, Silver, Yellow, Red, Blue or Emerald), so they need those games imported; the FACETS screen says when one is missing. Nothing from any game is included.

## Compatibility

- Mod API 2, gen1recomp 0.3.91 or newer. Gen 3 games only.
- `content` profile: link play is unaffected.
- Works alongside Emerald Lens and SPRITE SWAPPER (and can make SPRITE SWAPPER packs from its sets).
