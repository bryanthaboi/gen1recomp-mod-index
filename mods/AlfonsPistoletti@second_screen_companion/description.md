# Second Screen Companion

See your game on your phone, tablet or TV while you play: your team, your
bag, the map, wild Pokémon nearby and every battle, live, in any web
browser. There's no app to install.

Works with **Gen1**, **Gen2** and **Gen3**.

## What it does

- **Your team at a glance:** every Pokémon with its HP, status, level, held
  item, stats and moves, updated as you play, even mid-battle. Eggs show
  the steps they still need to hatch.
- **Battle helper:** the foe's HP and the moves it has used, the trainer's
  team, stat changes, weather, and effects like Leech Seed or Reflect with
  the turns left.
- **DexNav:** which wild Pokémon live where you're standing, at what level
  and how often, with the ones you've caught marked.
- **Maps:** the area you're in with its exits and items, and the town map.
- **Pokédex:** every entry with pictures, descriptions, evolutions, moves,
  where to find it, its cry and its footprint.
- **Your bag and PC:** see everything, and sort or rearrange it from your
  phone. Use Potions, Rare Candy, stones, TMs, the bike and rods, and use
  Cut, Surf, Fly and other field moves with one tap.
- **Shiny hunting helpers:** a reset counter, shiny egg odds, and your
  active shiny chain with supporting mods.
- **Extras per game:** the Pokégear in Gold, Silver and Crystal (calls and
  radio from your phone); contests, berry trees and rematches in Ruby,
  Sapphire and Emerald.
- **TV dashboard:** one big overview for a TV or second monitor nobody
  touches, with pop-ups for catches, level-ups and badges.

## Install

1. Download `second_screen_companion-<version>.zip` from the
   [releases page](https://github.com/AlfonsPistoletti/gen1recomp-mod-second-screen-companion/releases).
2. In the launcher, MODS → **Import mod .zip**, then enable it.
3. Start the game and open **OPTION > PHONE** (Ruby to Emerald:
   **START > PHONE**). Scan the QR code with your phone, or type the address
   shown on the game screen into any browser on the **same Wi-Fi**.

On Windows, allow the firewall prompt for **private networks** the first
time.

## Works with other mods

New Pokémon, items, maps and translations from other mods show up too.
Extra support for **Kanto Reforged**, **1025dex**, **Untamed Tohjo**,
**Untamed Advanced**, **Modern Spawns** with **Wilds of Hoenn**, and
**Wilds of Kanto Revival**.

## Compatibility

- Mod API 2. Pure `content` profile: link play is unaffected.
- Permissions: `network` (the page is served on your own network only, not
  the internet) and `engine_internals`.
- Turn on **SECURE MODE** in the mod options if others share your Wi-Fi.
