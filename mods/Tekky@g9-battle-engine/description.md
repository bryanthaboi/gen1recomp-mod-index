Gen 9 battle engine rewire to gen 2 and to gen 1 in the future.

This mod seeks to be a battle framework and requires to be used alongside national dex mod that feeds it the moves, abilities and pokemon themselves.

update game to accept the 934 moves and their effects, abilities, weathers, the 4 gimmicks, EV, IV, Special Split, Special/Attack split, alternate forms, updated turn mechanics and double battles, also updated held items.

future scope: Dynamax/Mega/Tera/Alpha Adventures and Dens.
