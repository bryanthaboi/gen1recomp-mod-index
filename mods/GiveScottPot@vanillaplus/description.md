Vanilla+ is a spoiler-light expansion for Pokémon Red, Blue, and Yellow on Gen1Recomp++. It focuses on quality-of-life improvements, expanded encounters, field tools, additional NPC and world interactions, and optional gameplay conveniences while preserving the feel of the original Gen I games.

Features include Running Shoes, Hidden Stats/DV viewing, EXP progress display, caught indicators, expanded encounter options, persistent Cut trees, the Adventurer’s Toolkit, additional NPC interactions, and individually configurable features.

Vanilla+ supports Red, Blue, and Yellow. Compatibility with other mods may vary when multiple mods hook the same controls or engine systems.

Install by downloading the latest Vanilla+ release ZIP and importing it through Gen1Recomp++.
