# Voxel Characters

Voxel Characters gives Gen 1 overworld characters a voxel body built from their original pixels, with adjustable shape, shading, breathing, and sway.

## What it changes

- Adds depth to overworld character sprites while preserving their pixel-art silhouettes.
- Offers options for front and top shaping, ground shading, and subtle character movement.
- Works with compatible voxel host mods, including PotatoVoxel, Battle Art Voxel Fork, Dramaless Shape, and Dramatic Shape.

## Install

1. Download `voxel_characters-1.8.2.zip` from the [latest release](https://github.com/last-swagger/gen1recomp-voxel-characters/releases/latest).
2. In the launcher, MODS → **Import mod .zip**.
3. Enable Voxel Characters and at least one compatible voxel host.

## Compatibility

- Mod API 2; Gen 1 games; engine `0.0.0-dev || >=0.1.51 <2.0.0`.
- Content profile; does not affect link play.
- Requires a compatible voxel host mod.
