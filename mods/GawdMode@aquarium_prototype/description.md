Angler's Cove transforms Cerulean City's old Bike Shop into a new fishing and aquarium side activity for Pokémon Crystal.

Catch aquatic Pokémon with an expanded fishing system, build your own living aquarium, care for its inhabitants, discover unusual catches, and earn rewards as your collection grows.

## Features

- Fishing 2.0 with visible Pokémon, casting, bites, and active reeling
- A persistent aquarium that can hold and display your caught Pokémon
- Feed, clean, and maintain your tank
- Individual Pokémon sizes and collectible catches
- Aquarium happiness and interactive Pokémon behavior
- Tank items including toys, Growth Pellets, and Love Powder
- Aquarium breeding and egg hatching
- Visiting collectors interested in valuable catches
- Remote Tank access
- Optional PokeSurvive integration, including randomized fishing encounters, type-based aquarium colors, Energy costs, and the Fishy mood

Designed as a self-contained side activity that expands fishing and gives aquatic Pokémon a place to call home.
