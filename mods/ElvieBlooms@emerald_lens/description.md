Play every game as someone new.

Emerald Lens swaps the player's sprites in all eleven games: FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald, and Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal. In the Gen 3 games it covers everywhere the game draws them: walking, running, biking, surfing, diving and fishing, field moves, your battle back pic and throw, the front pic, the trainer card, the new-game intro, the map icon, Fly and the Mt. Chimney cable car. In the Game Boy games your look walks, bikes, surfs and fishes in full colour, with its battle pics, Oak's intro, the Hall of Fame and the Gold/Silver/Crystal trainer card.

- **45 looks over 11 characters**, Game Boy, Gen 3 and Gen 4 (HGSS) style, and you can add your own. Missing art falls back to the character's other looks, sheet by sheet.
- **A rival of your choosing**, with their text following the look: granddaughter, she or they.
- **Text that matches your character**, girl or enby, where the game assumes a gender.
- **Pokémon to ride while surfing**, and Yellow's surfing Pikachu.
- **A LENS screen** on the START menu to pick it all, with a preview.
- **Achievements** of its own with Recomp Achievements installed.

It's the companion to [Crystal Lens](https://github.com/dburton95/crystal) (listed here as Crystal Player Mod), built on its framework with Dgray66's permission, and it uses the same character folders. With both installed, Crystal Lens leads in the Game Boy games and Emerald Lens's looks join its lists. Sprite tools to make your own looks are at [elvieblooms.github.io/emerald_lens](https://elvieblooms.github.io/emerald_lens/).
