Fast Nurse Joy
Talk to Nurse Joy and your party is healed immediately -- no dialogue, no YES/NO prompt. The healing sound still plays, because the heal and the sound are the game's own; this mod only suppresses her text boxes and answers her prompt for you.

Works at every Pokemon Center, including ones you have never visited. No other NPC or script in the game is affected.

Install
Mods panel -> Import mod .zip -> select this file. Enable it for Gold.

Compatibility
Built and confirmed working on Gen1Recomp v0.2.15 (Gold / Gen 2). Confirmed in Goldenrod and Route 32 Pokemon Centers.
