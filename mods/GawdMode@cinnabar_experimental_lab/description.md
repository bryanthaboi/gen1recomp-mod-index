Mutant Monster Lab adds an experimental Pokémon research facility to Cinnabar Island. Fund five tiers of increasingly dangerous research, splice persistent mutations into individual Pokémon, and manage Fusion Stability as repeated experiments risk catastrophic results.

At Tier 5, restore the Holon Collider, which periodically rolls three global phenomena that can radically alter wild Pokémon with effects such as randomized types, stats, movesets and palettes, Lv100 encounters, shinies, shuffled species, Evolution Cascade, Limit Break up to Lv255, and more.

Mutations persist through PC storage, saving, reloading and supported evolutions. Includes compatibility support for PokeSurvive, Angler's Cove Aquarium, and Safari Derby.

Install through the Gen1Recomp mod manager/launcher for Pokémon Crystal.
