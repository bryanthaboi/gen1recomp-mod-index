Shortcuts for the battle menu: moves and battle actions mapped straight to
your controller.

The command step puts FIGHT, RUN (held), POKEMON and PACK on the four face
buttons, drawn with the glyphs of the pad in your hands -- Xbox letters,
PlayStation shapes, Nintendo letters, detected live. FIGHT opens the move
compass: the d-pad is the four moves, one press per slot, with PP and type
on each card. Both sticks drive radial selection, and while the foe is
still attacking the compass stays live underneath the text, so the next
action queues and fires the instant the menu returns.

Every choice runs the vanilla battle code -- ghost battles, trapping locks,
Struggle, disabled moves and forced switches all behave exactly as they
always did, on Red, Blue, Yellow and Gold. Safari, the old-man demo, Mimic
and the Bug-Catching Contest keep the classic UI on purpose, and keyboard
players keep everything with the keys shown on the buttons themselves.
