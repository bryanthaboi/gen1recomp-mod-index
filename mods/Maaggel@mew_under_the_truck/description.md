Everyone knew someone whose cousin had done it. Use STRENGTH on the truck by the S.S. Anne and MEW is underneath.

It was never true. The Vermilion Dock ships with no bg events and no object events at all - there has never been anything there to interact with, which is exactly why the rumour had room to grow.

This mod makes it true, without pretending the rest of the game never happened.

## Getting back to the dock

In vanilla, once the S.S. Anne sails the sailor turns you away and the dock is gone for good. That is the real reason the legend was untestable: by the time most players thought to try, they could not reach the truck at all.

Here he tells you the ship has sailed but the dock is still open, and offers to let you down. Decline and he asks again next time - it is never a lockout. He says the same thing if you talk to him directly.

Before the ship departs nothing changes. His S.S. Anne Ticket check is reproduced unchanged.

## The truck

Face it and you are told what everyone actually found in 1996: it's an old truck, and it won't budge.

If you hold the RAINBOWBADGE and can use STRENGTH, you get the choice the rumour always promised. The truck itself never moves - only the text says it shifts.

MEW is level 5, and is retired only once you genuinely catch it. Faint it, let it flee, or black out, and the truck stirs again next time.

## Requirements

The RAINBOWBADGE plus STRENGTH - either taught to a party member, or simply owned in the bag if you also run **HM Anywhere**, which this detects and defers to.

Reaching the dock afterwards needs SURF, since the vanilla departure script floods the lower half of the map. That is untouched here.

## Install

Download the release .zip and use **MODS > Import mod .zip** in the launcher, or drop the `mew_under_the_truck` folder into your `mods/` directory and restart. Content registries freeze at boot, so a full restart is needed.

## Notes

The S.S. Anne interior is deliberately left alone. Re-boarding lands you in maps whose story scripts have already run; the goal here is only that the dock stays reachable.

No ROM-derived content. Lua and text only.
