# Recomp Achievements Online

An optional add-on for [Recomp Achievements](https://github.com/ElvieBlooms/recomp_achievements). Every time you save, it sends your leaderboard card to the community leaderboard, if it changed. No more copying codes off the screen.

Recomp Achievements itself never goes online. This add-on is the only part that does, which is why it's a separate download: install it if you want automatic scores, leave it out to keep everything local.

## What you get

- **Scores on save.** Your leaderboard card and Extra Credit page go up when you save. The bottom of the card says ONLINE: SENT once it's there.
- **Link it to Discord.** Open the card (START > TROPHIES > SELECT) and press A for a code, then send `/link` and the code to the leaderboard bot. Your save's scores then belong to your Discord account, and Save Sync carries the link to your other devices.
- **Options** in the Mods manager: ONLINE SCORES, SEND EXTRA CREDIT and LINK CODE ON A.

## What it sends

To the leaderboard bot on Cloudflare: the game, trainer name, points, achievements earned, play time, save ID and check code from your card, the Extra Credit lines if that's on, and a random key kept in your save. Nothing is sent to RetroAchievements.

## Needs

Recomp Achievements 2.7.1 or newer, which the launcher installs with it. An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with RetroAchievements.
