# Trainer Tactics

A battle brain for FireRed and LeafGreen: it advises, or it plays.

- **Real numbers.** Every move's damage comes from the game's own formula (stats, stages, types, STAB, weather, abilities such as Levitate), as the lowest and highest roll in % of the target's HP, with accuracy and who moves first.
- **Advisor.** While you choose, a small box on the battle screen shows the best play; the Trainer Desk panel shows everything: your four moves, what the foe can do to you, and what each team mate would give and take.
- **Auto battle.** Switch it on and Tactics plays: it attacks, puts a healthy foe to sleep or paralyses it when there is no knock-out in sight, sets up or heals when there is time, switches out of a bad matchup, picks who comes in after a faint and answers "Will you switch?". Questions that are yours (a new move, a nickname) are left to you.
- **Fair-play option.** Foe moves can be read (default) or guessed from its types.
- **Works with the family.** Trainer Pilot uses the brain for its training and hunting battles; Trainer Controls gets actions to switch auto battle and advice on and off.

It does not use items. English, Spanish, Portuguese and Japanese. Tested on FireRed and LeafGreen with Gen1Recomp 0.4.8.
