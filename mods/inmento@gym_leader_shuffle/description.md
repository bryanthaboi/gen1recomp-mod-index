# Gym Leader Shuffle

Gym Leader Shuffle creates a persistent per-save gym-leader order for the eight Kanto gyms in Gen 1 and all sixteen Johto and Kanto gyms in Gold. The visiting leader supplies their presentation and battle identity, while the physical gym keeps its own badge, TM, rewards, and progression.

Optional trainer shuffle extends the assignment to eligible gym NPCs while retaining the physical gym’s intended level curve. Optional move and held-item settings provide additional variety. Gold includes leader dialogue, sprites, gym NPCs, held items, a spoiler log, Gym Warp, and Return Point actions.

> Example: if Lt. Surge visits Pewter Gym, Surge appears with a Pewter-scaled team; winning still awards Brock’s Boulder Badge and TM Bide.
