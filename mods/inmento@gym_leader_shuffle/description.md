# Gym Leader Shuffle

Gym Leader Shuffle creates a saved leader assignment for the eight Kanto gyms in Gen 1 and all sixteen Johto and Kanto gyms in Gold. The visiting leader provides the encounter presentation and battle identity, while the physical gym keeps its badge, TM, rewards, progression flags, and intended level curve.

## Features

The mod supports persistent per-save leader assignments, visiting-leader dialogue and sprites, destination-gym scaling, optional moves, eligible held-item handling, optional gym-NPC shuffling, spoiler information, and testing teleports. Gold includes its own compact options for all sixteen gyms.

## Install

Download the newest `gym_leader_shuffle-<version>.zip` from the [release page](https://github.com/inmento/Gym-Leader-Shuffle/releases). In Gen 1 Recomp, open **MODS**, choose **Import mod .zip**, import the archive, and enable the mod.

## Compatibility

The mod is **standalone**: it does not require a randomizer, Starter Picker, or any other mod. It can be used on its own, alongside a supported randomizer, or alongside Starter Picker. It targets API 2 and supports Gen 1 and Gold, using the engine’s active game version to choose its generation-specific branch. It declares the `engine_internals` permission for its presentation and battle integrations.
